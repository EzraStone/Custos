§ 01

Three editorial rules

Each exists because of a specific way reports like this fail.

SEC-20 Every finding names an owner, or it is segregated. A list a security lead cannot route is a list they stop opening. Unattributed candidates appear in their own section and are never mixed into the owned findings. Invariant
Evidence Every finding carries the sentences behind it. The workload owner will dispute the finding. They should be arguing with a byte ratio, not with a confidence score. Editorial
Limits Every limitation is stated in the document. What was not collected, how stale the catalogue is, what the spend figures are worth. A report that overstates once is never trusted again. Editorial

Findings are ordered by what each agent could destroy, not by how confident the classifier is that it exists. Blast radius is read from IAM policy: it states what the credential permits, not what the agent has done, and it is labelled that way.

§ 05

What this report does not claim

This section is part of the document, not a footnote to it.

  • Payload contents were never collected. Identities, endpoints, byte counts, timings, and protocol fingerprints are the entire input to every finding above (SEC-18).
  • Nothing in this report authorises anything. A discovered agent has no standing until an operator grants it explicitly (SEC-17).
  • The endpoint catalogue has a revision, and it goes stale. A provider endpoint added after revision 2026-08-18 would not be recognised, and an agent using only that provider would not appear here.
  • Blast radius is read from IAM policy, not from observed traffic. It states what the credential permits, not what the agent has done.
  • Spend figures use unverified placeholder pricing. They are valid only for ranking agents against each other. Do not reconcile them against an invoice.

And one more, stamped by the collector rather than the report: a scan with incomplete coverage says so on its face. An absence of findings under poor coverage is not the same as a clean account, and the difference is the whole meaning of the result.
